Dept. of Vehicular Wildlife Management

FIELD MANUAL

Standard operating procedure for the discerning motorist.
Committed to memory, ideally before the memorial.
Steering
Drag anywhere on the road. The van follows your finger the way liberty follows an open lane — promptly, and without asking why.
🦌 The Deer the entire point
Hit them. Each one enters the ledger, feeds the meter, and grows the streak. They will attempt to cross regardless. That is between them and their government.
Traffic does not count
Fellow motorists. Hitting one costs a heart and adds nothing to the ledger. They pay taxes. Allegedly.
🇺🇸 Flags four, no refunds
Lose all four and the Freedom Report is filed on your behalf. Pie restores one flag. The diner provides; see ORDNANCE.
The Meter 12 deer arms it
Fills along the top of the screen. At twelve: STAR-SPANGLED OVERDRIVE — nine seconds of speed and invincibility. Traffic becomes debris, though each car you total shortens the celebration. Past fifty deer, Overdrive also comes with meteors. See CAVALRY.
Streak resets on damage
Consecutive deer without taking a hit. Streaks summon the cavalry and unlock the ordnance. Damage resets it to zero, as the founders warned it would.
Combo ~2 seconds
Rapid hits stack: Double, Triple, Quadruple, Rampage. Worth nothing but style. Style is not nothing.
Fog
Rolls in on its own schedule. The world shrinks to one headlight cone, and the deer — this is documented — multiply in it. Drive like it's personal.
Black Ice from 30 deer
A dark patch, then the wheel goes light and the van votes independent. Steer through the skid like your grandfather taught you. Overdrive does not skid. Overdrive does not do a lot of things.
The Oncoming Rig from 70 deer
A semi, southbound, in your northbound. It honks once as a courtesy and yields never. Be elsewhere. This is the entire briefing.
🫎 The Moose formerly neutral · +12
Not prey. Not traffic. No longer non-negotiable. Three points of health, and the blade, the treeline, the rifle and the airburst all have jurisdiction now. The bumper still pays the old tax — that is not diplomacy, that is mass. The meteors still bounce off, and the moose still declines to notice.
The Country
The road crosses nine Americas — pines, desert, high country, bayou, farm, lakeside, the winter plains, downtown, and the subdivisions. Each has locals. Some assist. One throws boulders. The road itself never changes; it is the only thing here that doesn't.
Winter the seventh
Snow, black ice, the Pale Deer, and Santa moving at highway speed. His sleigh can hit you. Destroy the team quickly and presents scatter across the road as powerups. Hesitate and clear the lane.
🏙️ Downtown the eighth
Six lanes of city freeway and one of them is shut. An arrow board, a run of barrels, a sign at the tail, and no crew — the work has been ongoing since 1997 and will conclude when the republic does. Clip a barrel and the wheel goes, the combo dies, and half the streak goes with it. Drop the blade or run Overdrive and the closure becomes a suggestion.
🏡 The Subdivisions the ninth
A breather, and not a free one. This is where the herd actually lives — they are thicker here and they are not afraid of you, because they have watched you put out a recycling bin. Somebody's pickup is always reversing out of a driveway at eleven miles an hour. Ring the bell here and the whole neighborhood turns out.
The Near Miss traffic & rigs
Slip a car by a hand's width and the meter pays half a point. Slip the Rig and it pays a full one. The department does not endorse this. The meter is not the department.
The Ledger permanent record
Lifetime deer, carried across every drive, and a commission to match: Civilian Motorist, Deputized Driver, Road Warden, Lane Marshal, Highway Colonel, Interstate General, Undersecretary of the Herd, and — at seven thousand five hundred — Secretary of Vehicular Wildlife. Ranks are not revocable. Nothing here is.
Issued equipment. Earned, not given —
this department has a budget.
🎺 The Horn 7s cooldown
One blast. Civilian traffic pulls itself together and hunts deer on your behalf for a few patriotic seconds; the deer panic, which has never once improved a deer's decisions.
🔔 The Liberty Bell one ring per country
No longer a thrown object. The bell is a summons: one ring per country, and the country decides who answers. Pines and subdivisions send hunters out of the tree line in blaze orange. Desert sends troopers. High country sends the rams, twice. Bayou and farm send the dusters. Lakeside sends an engine and a cruiser. Downtown sends two engines and no paperwork. Winter sends the mountain itself. Spend it on the clump you cannot outdrive; it does not come back until the road crosses into the next America. Crack included at no charge.
🥧 Pie appears when hurt
One flag, à la mode. At full health it charges the meter instead — the Republic wastes nothing. It is on the road because someone believed in you. Probably the diner.
🎆 Shell Crate roadside · +2
Roadside, unattended, free. Two shells for the tube, and it comes around often enough that the department has stopped pretending it is an accident. This is either infrastructure or providence and the department declines to say which.
Grandfather's Rifle streak 8 · or 110 deer
Eight rounds, each good for two deer in a line, eighteen in the box. Has not missed since the Carter administration. Two on the sasquatch, two on the moose, two on the bird, two on the log with eyes — the season is open on everything. Aimed at traffic it wings the car into a violent spin, and be advised: roughly one motorist in three is armed and files a response. She rides the score as well as the streak, so a bad night in the fog does not cost you the season.
The Plow Blade streak 28
Nine seconds of blade, racked FOUR at a time — every award refills the full set. Deer are rezoned as behind the bumper; traffic is LAUNCHED, spinning, into whatever it lands on, and whatever it lands on goes too. Municipal equipment, liberated for federal use, returned in worse condition.
The Bottle streak 45 · or 180 deer
Doe estrus lure. Do not ask what is in it. Empties the tree line onto your road for seven and a half seconds. Use it somewhere open, and let the van build speed, not on ceremony.
Air Support 320 deer
Three B-2s and one favor owed. Two full screens of carpet policy. Friendly to you; a matter of record for everything else on the pavement.
🎆 The Firework Mortar three in the tube at the flag
A tube, a baseplate, and a shell with a fuse cut for mid-range. It opens where you can still see it — and then the sky keeps working: a dozen live echoes walk the road for four full seconds, each one clearing its own patch of herd, traffic, cryptid, gator and bird. Five shells maximum, and it reloads off the streak, off the score, off the crates, off Overdrive, and off the Patriarch's crown. You are meant to be firing this constantly. There is no longer anything else that solves the whole screen, so solve it with this.
Desk Reference keyboard
Arrows or A/D steer · ↓/S brake, held to a dead stop · SPACE horn · B bell · R rifle · V plow · G bottle · X air support · C mortar · P pause. The van accelerates on its own. Issued for motorists whose van is a desk.
Known parties, with bounty where applicable.
Values in deer, the only honest currency.
🦌 Common Deer +1
The base unit of the economy. Crosses without signaling.
🦌🦌 Twins +1 each
Travel as a pair, arrive as a queue. The lane abhors a crowd.
Does & Fawns +1 · every size
Hornless, and in every size the forest makes — from filing-cabinet does down to spotted fawns. No antlers, all audacity. The ledger counts them the same.
The Sprinter +2
Crosses at a dead run. It had plans. Lead your target.
The Heavy Buck +3
Built like a filing cabinet and moves like one. Worth three; feels like more.
✨ The Gold Deer +5
Faster, shinier, and legal tender in four states. Charges the meter like it owes you money.
🐇 The Jackalope +7
Real. Briefly. Zigzags on principle. The largest ordinary entry the ledger accepts on the hoof.
The Pale Deer +9 · from 110
Nine, and the ledger's highest entry. The cavalry cannot see it: rifle rounds pass through, fire finds nothing to burn, the eagles do not know it is there. It answers to the bumper and the blade alone, and only in the moments it consents to be solid — it fades roughly two seconds in every five, and a swing through a faded one is a swing through nothing. Seen most in winter and in fog. It has crossed this road before.
👑 The Patriarch every 100 deer
The elder of the herd and its entire legal team. Four hits to bring down — bell, rifle, redirected traffic, the cavalry, or your own bumper between his charges. Drops his crown, a heart, two shells, and a full Overdrive. Worth ten, plus the moral high ground.
The Sasquatch forest · +15
Upright, long-armed, and taller than your van. Three hits at first sighting, five once he is enraged — rifle, bumper, blade, mortar, the hounds, or the birds. Strike him and he does not leave; he turns. Put him down and the forest goes quiet and the ledger gains fifteen. He was here before the road, and that is the only argument he has left.
🐊 The Bull Gator bayou
A log with eyes and a schedule. Lunges across lanes; eats deer as a hobby and would eat van as a career. Three hits settles the matter — an airburst is worth two, a rifle round is worth two, the bell rings him once, and the blade will do it at contact range if you have the nerve to stay there. A hit mid-lunge sends him back to the shoulder. Settled, he is worth eight, in boots.
The Thunderbird 150+ deer · +12
Grips the van and steers you toward a worse future. Steer out, hard — or shoot: it is vulnerable to gunfire now. Two rifle rounds ground it, the treeline pecks at it, an airburst is very much its problem, and getting shot breaks its grip. Loud about everything.
The Lake Serpent lakeside
Count the humps, then mind the arc. She takes a deer under — no charge, no receipt — and anything beneath her when she lands. Do not be beneath her.
The Bighorn Ram mountain
Paws the gravel, picks a line, audits the whole lane. Clears deer generously. Clears vans by the same mechanism. Technically an ally; practically a wall with opinions.
Assets that ride when the streak runs hot.
Every one of their deer still counts to your ledger.
🦅 The Eagles streak 15
Descend on a streak, depart with deer, invoice in fireworks. The whole federal budget, in miniature.
🐻 The Biker Bears streak 30
A motorcycle chapter, riding through at speed. The herd does not enjoy their meetings. At streak sixty the full chapter turns around and comes back.
🟧 The Hunters the bell, in tree country
Blaze orange, no questions, no permits produced on request. They step out of the tree line with deep boxes and hold the shoulder for the better part of half a minute, and when the herd runs out they go up a weight class: sasquatch, moose, bird — nothing is exempt from the season. They will not enter your lane and they will not hit your van; the department has been very clear about the second one and merely hopeful about the first. Most of them are named Dale.
The State Trooper streak 12s
Rolls up, PIT-maneuvers deer at his own discretion, files no report. He saw nothing. Neither did you.
The Engine Company streak 15s
Brought the deck gun. Deer are pressure-washed off the road and onto the ledger. Municipal excellence, billed to no one.
The Crop Duster farm & bayou
Dusts the herd into a coughing standstill. Agriculture, weaponized. A stationary deer is a personal choice.
🛸 The Visitors desert
They also want the deer. Nobody knows why and it is considered rude to ask. Their abductions post to your ledger; occasionally they take a car instead, which is on them.
The Wolf Crate runs over easily
Government hounds in a wooden box, addressed to the middle of the road. Run the crate over; the hounds handle the paperwork. Do not ask which agency.
☄️ The Meteors Overdrive, 50+ deer
Past fifty, Overdrive comes with orbital cooperation. The heavens fly our colors and their aim is improving.
